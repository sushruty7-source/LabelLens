Classifications reflect IARC Monograph hazard groups, which describe strength of evidence that a substance can cause cancer under some condition of exposure — not the risk at the dose actually found in this product, and not a prediction about any individual. "N/A" is not the same as "safe": it just means no hazard evaluation exists yet, which is common for many additives. This prototype's reference table lives in carcinogen-table.json (~30 substances) — a curated demo subset, not the full IARC/Prop 65 database. Several entries note packaging or processing sources rather than added ingredients. Not medical or safety advice.
